title: How and why I made this blog
---
Will flesh this out later, but in short, thanks to the teams and people behind Quartz, Obsidian and GitHub, I'm able to create a website and add content with ease, and I retain control of the information. It's also easy to move around if I decide to no longer use any of those tools. Since the content itself is markdown files, they can be read on most devices, as well as easily ingested into any future systems.
