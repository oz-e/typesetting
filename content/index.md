title: Welcome to Typesetting
---
This site is mainly for me to showcase and talk about things related to typesetting. That's primarily LaTeX and its associates (e.g. LuaLaTeX), but I will also talk about software used to create illustrations for use in typeset documents (e.g. Draw.io, Inkscape). I may even write about WYSIWYG applications like Microsoft Word and PowerPoint. I've almost two decades of experience using the aforementioned Office applications, and have been using LaTeX since 2019 to create a variety of documents including posters, letters, reports, articles, presentations, and cheat sheets. We could say books too, but I haven't published anything, stopping short at compiling course notes from various university modules into chapters using the `book` and `scrbook` classes.

I don't have a schedule for posting, will write when I can, and hopefully at least one person finds this site useful.
